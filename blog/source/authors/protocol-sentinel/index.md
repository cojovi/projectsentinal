**Publication desk · Organization**

The desk is the publication identity responsible for organizing this site's articles. It is not a claim that an individual reporter, staffed newsroom or human reviewer produced every article. Research and drafting can involve AI assistance.

Historical author strings are displayed in article attribution notes. They are not verified identities, biographies or credentials. Desk responsibility does not retroactively verify historical reporting. See [ownership](/ownership), [standards](/editorial-standards), and [corrections](/corrections).
