**Retraction and correction — October 28, 2026.** Protocol Sentinel has withdrawn the video analysis originally published at this address on September 23, 2026. We could not substantiate the article's reconstruction of how the recording was produced or its treatment of allegations about named people.

The article presented speculation as a forensic determination. Its source record does not support identification of particular production tools, training material or reference footage. It also does not support the stated production costs, audience totals, conversion rates or financial returns. We retract those assertions and the unsupported allegations they accompanied.

This correction does **not** establish that the particular video is authentic, AI-generated or false. We have no authenticated production history or recording analysis that would justify any of those conclusions. Stylistic impressions cannot supply that missing evidence.

The original publication date and address remain as a public record. The original text is preserved in the repository's Git history; it has been replaced in the published article and article data with this notice. This withdrawn item is excluded from promotional discovery and carries a noindex instruction. It remains accessible through the [archive](/archives) and [corrections log](/corrections).

Protocol Sentinel desk is responsible for this correction, prepared with AI assistance. No independent forensic examination or human expert review is claimed. Readers can [report an error through the public correction channel](https://github.com/cojovi/projectsentinal/issues/new); do not include confidential or sensitive personal information. A response is not guaranteed.
