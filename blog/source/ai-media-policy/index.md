---
title: "AI assistance and illustration policy"
date: 2026-10-28 12:00:00
updated: 2026-10-28 12:00:00
---

AI tools can assist research, drafting and editing. Output is not evidence merely because it sounds confident. Recorded sources, unknowns and reporting cutoffs belong in the Evidence and Limits box. Historical tool use may be incomplete; no universal human-review claim is made.

Synthetic retro pixel art is editorial illustration, not a photograph, leaked screenshot, measured output, scene reconstruction or evidence that an event occurred. Visible credits describe the recorded creator, production method and limits. Where creator or production method was not recorded, the credit says so rather than guessing.

A proposed test remains a proposal until its methods, artifacts and results exist. We do not present planned evaluations as completed hands-on testing. See [standards](/editorial-standards).
