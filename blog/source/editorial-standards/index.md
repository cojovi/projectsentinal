Protocol Sentinel explains mechanisms, incentives and uncertainty. Reporting should distinguish primary documents, attributed accounts, vendor assurances, inference and opinion. A company announcement establishes what the company says; it is not an independent audit. Several reports repeating one account do not supply several confirmations.

Articles identify their publication date, substantive updates, reporting cutoff and evidence limits. A documented source check covers the specified claims and sources. It does not certify the whole article, authenticate every recording or imply a human expert reviewed it. Historical articles awaiting reassessment carry a source-review-pending label.

Research, drafting and corrections may use AI assistance. The Protocol Sentinel desk retains publication responsibility. Historical bylines remain credited as records; they do not establish qualifications or a current reviewer. We do not claim independent field reporting or replicated tests without the underlying work.

Link the actual supporting documents, attribute quotes and measurements, and record unresolved questions. Describe illustrations as illustrations. Northstar Ledger shares ownership with this publication; a sister-site link is not independent corroboration.

Substantive corrections receive dated notes explaining what changed and why. Withdrawn work keeps an accessible correction record at its original address and is removed from promotional discovery.
